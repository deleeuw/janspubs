This website (created with bibtex2html) provides access to my archive of published and unpublished articles, reports, books, presentations, and packages.

There are about 700 entries, arranged from new to old, starting in 1965. If there are published and unpublished versions of a paper, then only the published version is in the archive, even though the two versions may be quite different.

For all entries in the archive there is a link to a bibtex description. In over 90 percent of the entries there is also a link to a pdf. Some entries point to books, and for some books I do not have pdfs. Also some unpublished papers are unfortunately lost. Some of these lost papers may still show up somehow. If you happen to have one, send me a pdf. Thanks.

You will also find that many of the unpublished papers are unfinished and incomplete, either because I got stuck in the middle or because I lost interest and started working on something else. They may still have some use.
