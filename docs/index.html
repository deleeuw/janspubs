This website (created with bibtex2html) provides access to my archive of published and unpublished articles, reports, books, presentations, and packages.

There are about 700 entries, arranged from new to old, starting in 1965. If there are published and unpublished versions of a paper, then only the published version is in the archive, even though the two versions may be quite different.

For all entries in the archive there is a link to a bibtex description. In over 90 percent of the entries there is also a link to a pdf, but some unpublished papers are unfortunately lost. Some of these lost papers may still show up. If you happen to have one, send me a pdf. Thanks.

You will also find that many of the unpublished papers are unfinished and incomplete, either because I got stuck or because I lost interest. They may still have some use.

Some entries point to books, and for some books I do not have pdfs. I am not going to copy the 600 pages of the published version of Gifi. But there are other ways to find books.
