This website (created with bibtexconv) provides access to my archive of published and unpublished articles, reports, books, presentations, and packages.

Click here for the archive.

There are about 700 entries, arranged from new to old, with the oldest files from 1965.

If there are published and unpublished versions of a paper, then only the published version is in the archive, even though the two versions may be quite different.

For about 90 percent of the entries there is a link to a pdf, but some unpublished papers are unfortunately lost. Some of these lost papers may still show up somehow. If you happen to have one, send me a pdf. Thanks.

You will also find that many of the unpublished papers are unfinished and incomplete, either because I got stuck somewhere or because I lost interest and started working on something else. They may still have some use.
